Notification Sound Manager

Custom notification sounds and individual volume controls for Linux.

Download Latest Release View on GitHub

Installation

Download the latest .deb package from the Releases page, then install it with: 

sudo apt install ./notification-sound-manager_<version>_all.deb
Important

Keep desktop notifications enabled in applications you want Notification Sound Manager to handle. Disable the application's own notification sound if you want to avoid hearing two sounds at once.

What is Notification Sound Manager?

Notification Sound Manager is a lightweight desktop application for Debian and Ubuntu-based Linux systems that lets you choose your own sounds and volume levels for different types of notifications.

General Notifications

Choose a custom sound and volume for normal desktop notifications.

Discord

Give Discord notifications their own custom sound and volume.

Email

Separate notification sounds for supported email clients.

Supported Email Clients

Thunderbird, Evolution, KMail, Geary, Mailspring, and Claws Mail.

Login

Play your chosen sound when you log into your desktop session.

Privacy

Notification Sound Manager runs locally on your computer. It does not require an account, cloud service, telemetry, or an internet connection during normal operation.
