What is Notification Manager?

Notification Manager is a lightweight desktop application for Debian and Ubuntu-based Linux systems that lets you choose your own sounds and volume levels for different types of notifications.

General Notifications

Choose a custom sound and volume for normal desktop notifications.

Discord

Give Discord notifications their own custom sound and volume.

Email

Separate notification sounds for supported email clients.

Login

Play your chosen sound when you log into your desktop session.

Supported Email Clients

Thunderbird, Evolution, KMail, Geary, Mailspring, and Claws Mail.

Installation

Download the latest .deb package from the Releases page, then install it with: 

sudo apt install ./notification-manager_<version>_all.deb

Privacy

Notification Manager runs locally on your computer. It does not require an account, cloud service, telemetry, or an internet connection during normal operation.

Important

Keep desktop notifications enabled in applications you want Notification Manager to handle. Disable the application's own notification sound if you want to avoid hearing two sounds at once.

Project Status

Notification Manager is actively being developed. Feedback, bug reports, and suggestions are welcome.
